Common questions

How do I find and add a calculator?

Open Marketplace in the app, find a calculator, and add it to My calculators. You can manage, reorder, or favorite the tools in your collection. Removing a calculator from the collection keeps its saved calculations and preferences.

Where are my saved calculations?

Open Saved to find calculations and project folders. Reopening a saved calculation restores its original inputs. If you calculate and save again, DivE creates another saved entry rather than replacing the original result.

How do I delete a saved calculation?

Use the delete action for the individual calculation in Saved. Removing a project folder keeps the calculations inside it. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data, but device backups and copies you have shared may remain elsewhere. We cannot retrieve or remotely delete records stored only on your device.

Can I use metric measurements?

Construction calculators offer an Imperial/Metric selector. Imperial dimensions use separate feet, inches, and fraction controls. Recipe scaling can convert compatible units or switch all results to metric together; converting weight to volume requires ingredient-specific information and is not part of that feature.

How do I change the clock format or appearance?

Time & work hours lets you choose System, 12-hour, or 24-hour display. Appearance and other personalization options are available in Customization. Tip presets can have their own labels and emojis.

How do purchases and restoring access work?

DivE uses Apple’s purchase system. Use Restore Purchases in the app with the Apple Account associated with the original purchase. Purchase offerings are still being developed; local test purchases do not represent live products or charges. If a restore fails, send us the app version and the message shown, without including payment information.

How can I change my ad privacy choices?

When Google requires an in-app privacy-options entry point, DivE displays an “Ad privacy choices” button in its settings. Your available choices depend on your region and Google’s configuration. Read the advertising section of the privacy policy for more information. Apple’s own ad-personalization setting does not control Google AdMob.

Can I rely on a calculator for a final decision?

Check inputs, units, rounding, and assumptions before using a result. Construction outputs are estimates and layout aids, not structural or building-code advice. Work-hour earnings are gross estimates using your entered overtime rules, not payroll or tax calculations. The date calculator’s weekday option does not remove public holidays.

Privacy questions

Please email us privately about your data or a privacy concern. Avoid posting personal information in public GitHub issues. Our privacy policy explains local storage, third-party services, and your choices.
