What this policy covers

DivE is an everyday and construction calculator app being developed by Mustard Seed Studios. This policy describes the current development preview and this website. DivE is not currently available as a public App Store release. Features and purchase options described as planned elsewhere on this site are not promises about an available release.

The current preview uses Google test advertisements and development purchase configurations. Test advertisements can still involve communication with Google. We will review this policy as the app and its services change.

At a glance

  • Your calculations are performed on your device. A DivE account is not required.
  • Saved calculations, projects, notes, and preferences are stored locally. DivE does not upload this content to a server operated by Mustard Seed Studios.
  • You decide when to share calculation text. Device backups may also include local app data.
  • Google advertising services and Apple purchase services have their own data practices. Local calculation storage does not mean that every app feature operates without a network connection.

Information stored on your device

When you choose to save a calculation, DivE stores its inputs, result, title, saved date, and formatting information. You can also add a project name and notes. Depending on the calculator, saved content may include measurements, prices, work schedules and earnings estimates, dates, or recipes and ingredients.

The app also stores preferences such as your chosen calculators and favorites, appearance, accent colors, tip presets and emoji choices, measurement units, and clock format. Development builds may retain temporary feature-unlock settings.

The current app does not provide a DivE account or its own cloud synchronization service. It does not request access to GPS location, contacts, a camera, or your photo library to perform calculations. Advertising services may nevertheless estimate general location from an internet connection, as described below.

Sharing and device backups

Sharing is initiated by you through your device’s sharing options. A shared calculation can include its result and relevant inputs. A shared saved entry can also include its title, saved date, project name, and notes. The app or recipient you choose receives that text and handles it under its own practices. The tip calculator also lets you copy result text to the clipboard.

Local app information may be included in device or iCloud backups, depending on your settings. Backups are managed by you and Apple, separately from DivE. See Apple’s explanation of iCloud Backup.

Google advertising and privacy choices

DivE integrates Google Mobile Ads, also known as AdMob, and Google’s User Messaging Platform for advertising privacy choices. When these services are used, Google and participating advertising providers may process technical and advertising information, including IP addresses, general location inferred from IP, device or app identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information.

This information can be used to deliver and measure advertising, support personalization where permitted, prevent abuse, and maintain or improve the services. The data processed depends on the services, configuration, permissions, and privacy choices that apply. DivE does not send your saved calculations, project names, or notes as part of its ad requests.

The app uses Google’s consent tools before requesting ads when required. When Google requires an ongoing privacy-choice entry point, an Ad privacy choices option is available in the app’s customization settings. Device tracking permissions, where applicable, are managed in iOS settings; they do not replace every other advertising privacy choice.

Ad-free access affects which ads are shown. It does not remove Apple’s purchase verification, erase information already processed by a provider, or guarantee that the app makes no network requests.

For more information, read the Google Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

Apple purchase services

DivE uses Apple’s in-app purchase system. The app receives and verifies purchase information, such as the purchased product and its ownership status, to recognize access and restore eligible purchases. Mustard Seed Studios does not receive your payment-card details through this purchase flow.

Current development purchase configurations are used for testing and do not mean that an offer is publicly available. Apple handles its account, payment, and purchase records under App Store & Privacy. Deleting a local calculation does not delete Apple’s purchase records.

Keeping and deleting your information

Saved calculations remain in local app storage until you delete them or that storage is removed. You can open a saved calculation and choose Delete saved calculation. Removing a project folder keeps its calculations in the main saved list.

Deleting information in DivE does not remove copies you shared, copies in existing backups, support emails, or records held by Apple or Google. Those copies are managed separately. Mustard Seed Studios does not have a server copy of your saved calculations to retrieve or delete for you.

For a privacy question or a request concerning information you sent to us, email mustardseedstudios@proton.me. Provider-held information is subject to the relevant provider’s controls and retention practices.

If you contact us

If you email Mustard Seed Studios, we receive your email address and the message or attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond and investigate the issue you describe. The correspondence is held in our support email account and is separate from the app’s local calculation storage.

Use mustardseedstudios@proton.me for privacy questions. Please do not put private information into a public GitHub issue.

This website and external links

This is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages. We do not add website analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, or cookies. The hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address and request details to deliver and protect the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to Apple, Google, GitHub, and other websites take you to services with their own privacy practices. Following a link or sending an email can share information with those services.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as DivE develops or its data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. This development-preview policy will be reviewed before a public app release.
