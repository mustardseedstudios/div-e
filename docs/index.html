A calmer way to calculate

Useful by default.
Personal by choice.

DivE started with a simple tip calculator. The same idea carries forward: keep the everyday controls easy to reach, and let the details adapt to you.

  1. 01

    Build your own collection.

    Explore the in-app Marketplace and add the calculators you want. Your personal list stays focused on the tools you actually use.

  2. 02

    Make it feel familiar.

    Choose your appearance and accent color. Personalize tip presets and emojis. Use metric or imperial measurements and your preferred clock format where they apply.

  3. 03

    Pick up where you left off.

    Save results with a project and note. Reopen the original inputs later, or choose what to share using your device’s sharing options.

A few useful answers

Before you
dive in.

Can I download DivE yet?

Not yet. The expanded DivE app is in development and is not currently available on the App Store. This site introduces the work in progress and provides privacy and support information.

Will the calculators be free?

The planned launch includes free essentials and optional purchases for construction specialists, individually or in a fixed bundle. Free essentials are planned to have banner ads, with an optional one-time purchase to remove banners from each calculator permanently. Final offers and local prices will be shown in the app when available.

What kind of ads are planned?

Banner ads only on free essentials. The launch plan excludes pop-up, fullscreen, and rewarded ads. Paid construction tools will be ad-free. This is the intended launch model; development builds currently use Google test ads while the older advertising system is being replaced.

Do I need an internet connection?

The calculator math and local saved work function without an internet connection. Advertising, purchase and restore requests, support links, and any sharing destination may need a connection.

Can I suggest another calculator?

Yes. Tell us what you are trying to work out and what would make it easier. Get in touch with Mustard Seed Studios. Please leave personal or sensitive information out of examples.
